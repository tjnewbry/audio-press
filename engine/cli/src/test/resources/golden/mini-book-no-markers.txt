Chapter One

The lighthouse keeper woke before dawn, as he always did, and climbed the hundred-step stair to check the lamp at the top of the tower.

Fog had rolled in from the sea during the night. Far below him a fishing boat crept along the coast, its distant engine the only sound in the grey morning. He watched it for a long time, until the boat was no more than a shadow, and then he went back down to make his breakfast of bread and strong black tea.

The supply ship was due at noon. He had no use for the radio. See example.com now.

He had three jobs to do before noon:

the lamp,

the logbook, and

the stores.

The ship brought letters, flour, and a crate of Anglo-Saxon history books for the long winter.

The end.
